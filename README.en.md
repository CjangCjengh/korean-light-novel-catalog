# Korean Original Light Novel Catalog

[Browse](https://cjangcjengh.github.io/korean-light-novel-catalog/?lang=en) · [한국어](README.md) · [中文](README.zh.md)

264 Korean original light novel series published in volumes, ordered by first-volume publication date. Includes authors, illustrators, imprints, volume counts, synopses, individual covers, and sources.

Filter by publication status, imprint, year, volume count, author, illustrator, side stories, drama CDs, and completion of the web version. Search links can be shared and results exported as CSV. Saved works stay in the current browser.

Data as of 2026-10-25. Dates refer to the first and latest published volumes. Unfinished series without a new volume for over two years are marked “Stalled.” Web completion is shown only when the print series is unfinished.

The catalog is in `data/catalog.json`, with covers in `covers/`. Sources are linked by work and volume. Chinese and English titles include translations made for this catalog.
